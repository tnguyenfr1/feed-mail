Feed Mail: privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Feed Mail is a private, personal app. It runs on its owner's own home server and is only reachable from the owner's own devices. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Feed Mail connects to the owner's email accounts (including Gmail) and calendars (including Google Calendar) to show the owner's messages and events in one place, send replies the owner writes, and answer meeting invitations the owner chooses to answer.

What it stores

Emails and calendar events stay on the providers' servers. Feed Mail reads them on demand and keeps them only in memory while the app is running. It stores only the sign-in credentials needed to connect, encrypted, on the owner's home server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Data from Google APIs is used only to provide the features above, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps & services) or by removing the account inside Feed Mail.
